Data Structures has been retired. It mixed things that belong to machine learning with things that
merely appear in it, and the split is clearer now:

- Arrays, matrices and sparse structures → [Numerical & Scientific Computing](/scientific-computing#arrays-linalg)
- Arrow and columnar formats → [Data & Dataframes](/data#lakehouse)
- Nearest-neighbor and vector indexes → [Retrieval, Embeddings & Search](/retrieval#vector-search)

General-purpose graph and tree crates are not on this site. They are good crates; they are not
machine learning, and [crates.io](https://crates.io/) indexes them better than we can.
