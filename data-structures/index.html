Data Structures has been retired. It mixed things that belong to machine learning with things that merely appear in it, and the split is clearer now:

  • Arrays, matrices and sparse structures → Numerical & Scientific Computing
  • Arrow and columnar formats → Data & Dataframes
  • Nearest-neighbor and vector indexes → Retrieval, Embeddings & Search

General-purpose graph and tree crates are not on this site. They are good crates; they are not machine learning, and crates.io indexes them better than we can.
