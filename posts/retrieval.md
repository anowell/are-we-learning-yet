Search infrastructure is a corner where Rust is the implementation language rather than a binding:
the vector databases, the full-text engine underneath hybrid search and the local embedding servers
on these shelves are Rust programs, and they publish Rust clients alongside the Python ones most of
their users reach for.

Assembling them into a pipeline is the part you still write yourself.
